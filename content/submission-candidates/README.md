# Submission candidate intake

This folder is for curator notes about documents that may be worth archiving. Do not store submitted files here by default.

A candidate record should capture:

- Title.
- Publisher/source.
- Original URL.
- Archived URL if known.
- Why it appears deleted, hidden, altered, or hard to access.
- Why preservation may be in the public interest.
- Who suggested it and when, if appropriate to retain.
- Legal/privacy/safety concerns.
- Review status.

Use `templates/submission-candidate.yaml` as the starting point.
